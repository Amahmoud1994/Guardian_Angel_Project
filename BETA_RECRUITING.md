# Recruiting beta testers

**Application form:** https://guardianangel.at/beta
**Goal:** 12 testers minimum (Google's rule for the 14-day closed test); aim to invite **15–20** in case some drop out.

## How it works

0. **Make sign-up invite-only first.** On Render, set `BETA_INVITE_ONLY=true`. From then on, only applicants you **shortlist or invite** (plus your admin usernames and any emails in `BETA_ALLOWED_EMAILS`) can create an account. Anyone else who finds guardianangel.at sees "private beta — apply at /beta". Accounts that already exist keep working, and switching it on doesn't affect people logging in.
   - Testers must **register with the same email they applied with**. One account per invitation.
   - **Guardians don't need an account**: they get alerts and verification links by email. Only add someone to `BETA_ALLOWED_EMAILS` if they also want to use the app themselves.
   - At public launch, set `BETA_INVITE_ONLY=false`.
1. Post one of the messages below in groups where solo travelers, hikers and lone workers gather.
2. Applications arrive in the app: **Monitor tab → Beta Applicants**. Each applicant gets a confirmation email, and you get a score from 0 to 100:

   | Answer | Points |
   |---|---|
   | Use cases (solo travel, hiking, lone work score highest) | up to 25 |
   | How often they'll travel in the next month | up to 25 |
   | Has a guardian in mind | up to 20 |
   | Willing to fill in the one-week survey (in-app feedback only scores less) | up to 20 |
   | Wrote a thoughtful "why" | up to 10 |

   Applicants who don't have Android or aren't 18+ are marked not eligible. iPhone users are kept on an iOS waitlist. Applicants who won't commit to 14 days are rejected by the form.
3. When you have enough applications (wait 3–5 days after posting), tap **⭐ Shortlist top 12**. Review the list, decline anyone who looks wrong, and shortlist a few more to reach 15–20.
4. Tap **📋 Copy shortlisted emails**, then paste them into Play Console → **Test and release → Testing → Closed testing → Testers → Email list**, and save.
5. In Play Console, copy the **"Join on the web"** opt-in link (it shows once the closed test is approved), paste it into **✉ Invite shortlisted testers**, and tap **Send Invites**. Each tester gets an email with the opt-in link, the Play Store link and three first tasks.
6. The 14 days start counting once at least 12 testers have opted in. Check Play Console's tester count after 2–3 days and nudge anyone who hasn't joined.

**Tips:**
- Post in **3–5 groups**, spread across a couple of days. Reply to comments quickly; it lifts the post in group feeds.
- Always **ask group admins for permission first** if the rules mention self-promotion. Many allow it for free, non-commercial beta tests.
- Mention that it's free, takes 2 minutes to apply, and that you're a solo developer. People are generous with indie projects.
- Don't promise anything you can't keep (e.g. paid perks), and never describe it as an emergency service.

## Reddit

Pick subreddits that allow beta-test posts, such as **r/betatests**, **r/AlphaandBetausers** and **r/TestMyApp**. Large communities like r/solotravel and r/hiking usually don't allow promotion: read their rules or message the mods first.

**Title:** `[Android] Looking for 15 beta testers for a solo-travel safety app (check-ins + trusted guardians)`

```
Hi all, I'm a solo developer building Guardian Angel, a "dead man's switch" style safety app for solo travelers, hikers and people working alone.

- You plan a trip and pick trusted contacts ("guardians")
- You check in with a private code before a timer runs out
- Miss a check-in → reminder → warning → your guardians get an email with your route and last known location
- A duress code looks like a normal check-in but silently alerts your guardians
- Guardians can verify themselves, and you can save and schedule trips

It's a closed test on Google Play, so I need people to keep it installed for 14 days (Google's rule). Feedback is just in-app messages plus one short written survey after a week, no calls.

Apply here (2 minutes): https://guardianangel.at/beta

Honest feedback, including "I'd never use this because…", is exactly what I need. Thanks!
```

## After the beta starts

- **Day 2–3:** check how many testers have opted in (Play Console → Closed testing). Send a friendly reminder to anyone missing.
- **Day 7:** send the one-week survey from [PLAY_LISTING.md](PLAY_LISTING.md#one-week-survey-5-questions-eg-google-forms) to all testers. For those who give short answers, follow up by email with the optional follow-up questions in the same file.
- **Day 14+:** in Play Console, apply for production access. Thank your testers, and keep them updated when the app goes public.
- **Privacy:** applications are personal data. Delete the ones you don't need (🗑 in the list) within 3 months of the beta ending, as promised in the privacy policy.
